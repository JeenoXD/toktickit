## My Reflection
The AI-generated first drafts of my Lab 3 spec documents were a good starting point but required significant refinement to align with the strict constraints of the handout (e.g., no email resets, no user deletion, specific admin safety rules). The most valuable moment was catching the `requesterId` leakage in the API spec; the AI defaulted to Lab 2 patterns, and I had to actively enforce the new authenticated-session paradigm. Debugging the Prisma migration was also a key learning experience, as preserving Lab 2 data required careful foreign key remapping rather than a simple schema reset.

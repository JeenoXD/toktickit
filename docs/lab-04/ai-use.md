## My Reflection
_Draft for me to rewrite in my own words:_ The specification agent was most useful for turning a long handout into numbered, testable rules (BR-01 to BR-25, AC-01 to AC-19) and for pointing out the choices the handout leaves open. I still had to check that those choices made sense for our product. The coding agent was most valuable when it ran the full test suites, including Playwright at three screen sizes, after every change: several real problems only appeared there, not in unit tests. My main lesson is to treat my own earlier labs as untrusted too. The serious issues in this sprint were a security hole and schema drift left over from Lab 2 and Lab 3, and one of my Lab 3 tests.md rows claimed a test that did not exist.
